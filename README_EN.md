## Working Methodology

Labs and investigations follow a workflow based on the activities of a SOC analyst:

1. **Objective:** Define the security problem or investigation purpose.
2. **Environment:** Document the systems and tools used.
3. **Evidence Collection:** Gather relevant logs, events, and indicators.
4. **Analysis:** Examine the information and identify suspicious patterns.
5. **Findings:** Document observations and potential security implications.
6. **Conclusion:** Summarize the results and propose actions or recommendations.

The main focus is to understand the investigation process, work with technical evidence, and develop analytical thinking.
