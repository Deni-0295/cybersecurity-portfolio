INCIDENT RESPONSE ACTIONS
SOC-CASE-001
--------------------------

1. CONTAINMENT

---

Action:
Isolate the affected hosts from the network.

Hosts:
PC-023
PC-017
PC-041

Objective:
Prevent further communication with potentially malicious infrastructure and reduce the risk of lateral movement or additional activity.

2. EVIDENCE PRESERVATION

---

Action:
Preserve relevant evidence before deleting or modifying suspicious files.

Evidence to preserve:

* invoice.docm
* update.ps1
* invoice_update.exe
* PowerShell execution events
* Process creation logs
* Network connection logs
* Relevant email messages and headers

Objective:
Maintain evidence required to determine the attack chain, scope, and impact.

3. IOC HUNTING

---

Action:
Search the environment for known indicators.

Indicators:

* SHA-256: 9f86d081884c7d659a2feaa0c55ad015
* Filename: invoice.docm
* Filename: update.ps1
* Filename: invoice_update.exe
* IP: 198.51.100.25
* Process relationship: winword.exe → powershell.exe

Objective:
Identify additional hosts that may have received or executed the suspicious content.

4. EMAIL CONTAINMENT

---

Action:
Identify and remove the phishing email from affected mailboxes.

Subject:
Urgent Invoice 2026

Attachment:
invoice.docm

Objective:
Prevent additional users from opening the malicious or suspicious document.

5. USER NOTIFICATION

---

Action:
Notify affected users and provide guidance not to open or execute suspicious attachments.

Objective:
Reduce the possibility of additional execution through the same phishing campaign.

6. ANALYSIS

---

Action:
Investigate the suspicious document, PowerShell script, process tree, network activity, and created executable.

Objective:
Determine whether the hosts were compromised and identify the attacker's actions.

7. ERADICATION

---

Action:
After evidence preservation and analysis, remove or quarantine confirmed malicious files and associated artifacts.

Objective:
Remove the malicious components from affected systems.

8. RECOVERY

---

Action:
Restore affected systems to a trusted state and reconnect them to the network only after security validation.

Objective:
Return systems to normal operation while reducing the risk of reinfection.

9. MONITORING

---

Action:
Continue monitoring affected hosts and search for the identified IOCs.

Objective:
Detect recurring malicious activity or additional compromised systems.

# RESPONSE SUMMARY

The initial response should prioritize containment and evidence preservation.

PC-023, PC-017, and PC-041 should be investigated as suspicious hosts due to the observed Word-to-PowerShell execution chain.

PC-078 should be treated as potentially exposed because the suspicious document was present, but no execution was detected.

Immediate deletion of suspicious files should be avoided until relevant evidence has been preserved and analyzed.
