# Indicators of Compromise (IOCs)

## Case Information

**Case ID:** SOC-CASE-003
**Lab:** 03 — Phishing Analysis
**Environment:** Simulated phishing scenario

## 1. Email Indicator

* **Sender email:** [security@microsoft-support-alert.com](mailto:security@microsoft-support-alert.com)
* **Sender domain:** microsoft-support-alert.com
* **Assessment:** Suspicious sender domain that appears to impersonate Microsoft.

## 2. URL Indicator

* **URL:** https://microsoft-security-verification.example.com/login
* **Protocol:** HTTPS
* **Subdomain:** microsoft-security-verification
* **Registrable domain:** example.com
* **Path:** /login
* **Assessment:** The URL uses Microsoft-related wording in its subdomain but does not use Microsoft's official domain.

## 3. Phishing Indicators

* **Urgency:** Account suspension threatened within 24 hours.
* **Impersonation:** The message claims to represent Microsoft Security.
* **Credential theft risk:** The user is asked to verify their identity through a login link.

## 4. Limitations

This investigation uses simulated data for educational purposes. The indicators have not been validated against a real phishing campaign. No external links were opened.
