# SUMMARY

The identified IOCs are associated with a suspected phishing-based incident.

The strongest indicators are the shared "invoice.docm" file hash, the winword.exe → powershell.exe process relationship, the suspicious PowerShell script, the network connection, and the creation of "invoice_update.exe".
